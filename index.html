01

AI & education

AI-generated study tools

My master’s thesis compares rereading with non-interactive and interactive ChatGPT-generated study guides, measuring memory, comprehension, and learners’ judgments of their own understanding. The results suggest that the AI-generated materials did not improve test performance relative to rereading, while learners reported greater enjoyment and engagement. Interactive guides also reduced overconfidence and improved metacomprehension accuracy.

Master’s thesis · Archived on ProQuest · Psychonomics data blitz · November 20, 2026
